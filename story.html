CHAPTER 01

A Little Boba Lie

You're too sweet for me.

A few months into knowing each other, Kaylee had developed a rather convenient habit of being "in the area". And since she was already nearby, she'd offer to bring him boba.

What Davis didn't know was that "in the area" usually meant sitting at home, twenty-five minutes away, waiting to see if he'd say yes.

If he did, she'd head out, pick up the drinks, and drive over to see him. His work was so busy that sometimes they'd only get a few minutes together before he had to get back to it.

This continued for months, and somehow, he never caught on.

The best part? Neither of us even likes boba that much because it's too sweet!

CHAPTER 02

The First of Many Miles

Before the clock strikes 30: the final checklist.

Davis had always wanted to visit Japan before turning thirty. He often joked that he'd agreed to go with several different groups, yet somehow nobody ever actually made the plans.

So Kaylee asked the obvious question: "Why don't we just go ourselves?" Less than a year into dating, we packed our bags for two and a half weeks in Japan and South Korea.

People say traveling together can make or break a relationship. And in a rather fitting twist, our very first day in Japan also brought our first argument as a couple. The culprit? A misunderstanding about punctuality and navigating Japan's public transit.

But we worked through it. Davis admitted that he was actually relieved that we'd finally had our first disagreement, and even more relieved by how easily we were able to move past it.

As it turned out, traveling together didn't just bring us closer. It showed us that even when things didn't go according to plan, we could still make the best of the situation as a team. It only made us fall even more in love, both with each other and with discovering the world side by side.

And we've been exploring together ever since.

CHAPTER 03

The Best-Kept Secret

Forever began beneath an emerald sky

Before our vacation, we'd already looked at engagement rings together, so Kaylee was fairly certain a proposal was coming. In fact, she was so confident it would happen during the trip that she'd already told quite a few people.

While packing, she teased Davis, questioning how he planned to sneak a ring into our luggage. Our packing system was simple: Davis gathered everything, and Kaylee packed it, so it would be difficult for him to sneak it past her.

Davis awkwardly admitted that he didn't have the ring yet.

Cue the disappointmen. And a running joke throughout the beginning of the trip. Every beautiful destination became another opportunity for Kaylee to announce, "This would be such a beautiful place to propose... if only you had the ring."

Little did she know, the ring had been traveling with us all along. She even came dangerously close to discovering it twice while moving between cities: once inside Davis's rain jacket and again at the bottom of his camera bag's laptop compartment.

Davis had a plan to propose on our second evening in Koh Phi Phi before a surprise beachfront dinner. But the weather forecast had other ideas.

Our first night was overcast, but thankfully dry. Out on the horizon, fishing boats cast an extraordinary green glow across the clouds, turning the sky a color neither of us had ever seen before.

Kaylee was completely captivated.

Davis led her to a secluded stretch of the resort's beach, beneath that strange and beautiful green sky, and finally asked the question.

Surprise, he really did have the ring.
