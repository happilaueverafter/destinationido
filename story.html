CHAPTER 01

A Little Boba Lie

The things we do for love.

Before our first date, Kaylee had developed a rather convenient habit of being "in the area" whenever Davis was working. And since she was already nearby, she'd offer to bring him boba.

What Davis didn't know was that "in the area" usually meant sitting at home, twenty-five minutes away, waiting to see if he'd say yes.

If he did, she'd head out, pick up the drinks, and drive over to see him. His work was so busy that sometimes they'd only get a few minutes together before he had to get back to it.

This continued for months, and somehow, he never caught on.

The best part? Neither of us even likes boba that much.

just 25 minutes away...

CHAPTER 02

So, Let's Go.

One dream trip, two countries, and a memorable train ride.

Davis had always wanted to visit Japan before turning thirty. He often joked that everyone talks about going to Japan, but somehow, nobody ever actually plans the trip.

So Kaylee asked the obvious question: "Why don't we just plan it and go ourselves?"

Less than a year into dating, we packed our bags for two and a half weeks in Japan and South Korea. Korea, Kaylee's birth country, was added to the itinerary because, naturally, we'd already "be in the area."

People say traveling together can make or break a relationship. For us, it made us fall even more in love — with each other and with discovering the world side by side.

Although, in a rather fitting twist, our very first day in Japan also brought our first argument as a couple. The culprit? A misunderstanding about public transportation.

We figured it out. And we've been exploring together ever since.

and so the adventures began...

CHAPTER 03

The Proposal That Almost Wasn't a Surprise

One hidden ring, several close calls, and a sky that turned green.

Before our vacation, we'd already looked at engagement rings together, so Kaylee was fairly certain a proposal was coming. In fact, she was so confident it would happen during the trip that she'd already told quite a few people.

Then, while packing, she teased Davis about how he planned to sneak a ring into their luggage. Our packing system was simple: Davis gathered everything, and Kaylee packed it.

Davis awkwardly admitted that he didn't have the ring yet.

Cue the disappointment — and a running joke throughout the beginning of the trip. Every beautiful destination became another opportunity for Kaylee to announce, "This would be such a beautiful place to propose... if only you had a ring."

Little did she know, the ring had been traveling with us all along. She even came dangerously close to discovering it twice: once inside Davis's rain jacket and again at the bottom of his camera bag's laptop compartment.

By the time we arrived in Koh Phi Phi, Davis had a plan to propose on our second evening. But the weather forecast had other ideas.

Our first night was overcast, but thankfully dry. Out on the horizon, fishing boats cast an extraordinary green glow across the clouds, turning the sky a color neither of us had ever seen before.

Kaylee was completely captivated.

Davis led her to a secluded stretch of the resort's beach, beneath that strange and beautiful green sky, and finally asked the question.

And this time, he really did have the ring.
